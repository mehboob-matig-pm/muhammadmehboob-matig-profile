# Zendesk Ticket Response Samples

## Tier 1 — Order Status Inquiry

Customer: I placed an order 3 days ago and have not received any tracking information. Can you help?

Response:
Hi [Customer Name],

Thank you for reaching out. I have checked your order and can confirm it was successfully processed on [date]. Your tracking number is [tracking number] and you can track your shipment at [carrier link].

Your estimated delivery date is [date]. If you have any further questions please do not hesitate to contact us.

Best regards,
Support Team

---

## Tier 1 — Refund Request

Customer: I want to return my order and get a refund. How do I do this?

Response:
Hi [Customer Name],

Thank you for contacting us. I am sorry to hear the order did not meet your expectations.

You are eligible for a return within [X] days of delivery. To start your return please follow these steps:

1. Visit our returns portal at [link]
2. Enter your order number and email address
3. Select the item you wish to return and the reason
4. Print the prepaid return label and drop off at your nearest post office

Once we receive your return we will process your refund within 3 to 5 business days back to your original payment method.

Please let me know if you need any further assistance.

Best regards,
Support Team

## Tier 1 — Wrong Item Received

Customer: I received the wrong item in my order. I ordered a blue shirt but received a red one.

Response:
Hi [Customer Name],

I sincerely apologize for this error on our end. This is not the experience we want for our customers.

I have raised this with our fulfillment team and we will send you the correct item immediately at no extra cost. You do not need to return the wrong item.

Your replacement order number is [number] and you will receive a shipping confirmation within 24 hours.

Again I am very sorry for the inconvenience caused.

Best regards,
Support Team

## Tier 2 — Escalated Billing Dispute

Customer: I was charged twice for the same order. I want this resolved immediately.

Response:
Hi [Customer Name],

Thank you for bringing this to our attention and I completely understand your frustration. A duplicate charge is unacceptable and I want to resolve this for you right away.

I have reviewed your account and can confirm that two charges of [amount] were processed on [date]. I have immediately initiated a refund for the duplicate charge of [amount]. This will reflect on your statement within 5 to 7 business days depending on your bank.

I have also flagged this internally to prevent it from happening again. As an apology for the inconvenience I have added a [discount/credit] to your account.

Please do not hesitate to reach out if you need anything further.

Best regards,
Senior Support Team

---

## Tier 2 — Escalated Delivery Not Received

Customer: It has been 2 weeks and I still have not received my order. The tracking shows delivered but I never got it.

Response:
Hi [Customer Name],

I am truly sorry to hear this. I understand how frustrating it must be to have waited this long without receiving your order.

I have reviewed your tracking information and can see the carrier marked the package as delivered on [date]. In cases like this we first recommend checking with neighbors or any safe location the carrier may have left the package.

If you have already checked and the package is still not found I have opened a carrier investigation on your behalf with reference number [number]. This process typically takes 3 to 5 business days.

In the meantime I have also raised a replacement order for you which will ship within 24 hours so you are not left waiting. Your replacement order number is [number].

I will personally follow up with you on [date] with an update on the investigation.

Best regards,
Senior Support Team
