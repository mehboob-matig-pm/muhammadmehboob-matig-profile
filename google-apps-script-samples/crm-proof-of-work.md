## What Was Built

- Custom CRM replacing off the shelf tools that did not fit client workflows
- Lead management with custom pipeline stages per client
- Task and follow up tracking for sales and operations teams
- Custom reporting dashboards showing real time pipeline data
- Integration with existing client tools and data sources
- Role based access control for team members
- Built to scale as client teams grow

## Why Custom Over Off The Shelf

Standard CRMs charge for features clients never use. We build only what the client needs, structured around how their team actually works, at a fraction of the ongoing cost.

## Tech Stack Used

- Full stack web development
- Custom database design
- API integration
- AI assisted development for faster delivery and debugging
